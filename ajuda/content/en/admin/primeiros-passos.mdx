# First steps

ITS has just been installed, and you're the first person to sign in. With this guide, you can have everything ready for your organization in one afternoon.

## What does the installation wizard ask for?

On first access, ITS opens a wizard that asks for the organization name, the time zone, the default language (Portuguese comes selected, and you can choose English) and your administrator account. There's no default user or password, and the <Regra id="RN-43">wizard shows up only once</Regra>. The five factory roles follow the language you choose: in English, they start as Employee, Analyst, Manager, Results validator and Administrator.

## In what order do I set things up?

1. **Entities:** build the organization's structure, with sites and departments. Every idea belongs to an entity, and roles apply per entity. See [Entities and categories](/en/admin/entidades-e-categorias/).
2. **Categories:** import a ready-made set (**Safety + [QDCEP](/en/referencia/glossario/#qdcep)** or **Generic**) and adjust the names and colors.
3. **Users:** create the accounts and give each person the right role, in the right entity. See [Users, roles and scope](/en/admin/usuarios-e-perfis/).
4. **Settings:** idea code prefix, triage reply deadline, attachments, passwords and modules. See [Settings and identity](/en/admin/configuracoes/).
5. **Visual identity:** the organization's color, logo and font and, if you want, the sign-in screen. See [Customize the sign-in screen](/en/admin/tela-de-login/).
6. **Email**, if you want notifications to arrive by email too. See [Email](/en/admin/email/).

<Dica>
  You don't have to do everything before you start. With entities, categories and a few people registered, ideas can already come in; you can adjust the rest as people use it.
</Dica>

## What should I test before opening it to everyone?

- Submit a test idea and take it all the way to the end with an analyst. That way you check entities, roles and notifications.
- Agree on the triage reply deadline with the improvement team: people who submit ideas see it.
- Tell people where to find the Help: under **Information**, in your account menu (your photo or initials), and in the "?" on each screen.
