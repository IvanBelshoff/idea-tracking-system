# User custom fields

Does your organization need each person's employee ID, tax ID or department? In **Administration > User fields**, people with the permission to manage users create <Regra id="RN-193">up to 8 active fields</Regra>. No field comes out of the box: each organization creates its own.

<Atencao>
  Collect only what has a purpose. Every piece of personal data you store is a responsibility of the organization (data protection law).
</Atencao>

## What kinds of field are there?

- **Open**, where the person types and ITS checks the type: <Regra id="RN-194">text, number, date or masked text</Regra>. The mask can be CPF (Brazilian tax ID; ITS checks its digits), phone or your own format: `9` is a digit, `A` is a letter and `*` is a letter or digit. Everything else, including `0` and spaces, is fixed: an employee ID like `AB-1234` uses the format `AA-9999`, and a mobile number, `(99) 99999-9999`. The mask applies while the person types.
- **Closed**, where the person <Regra id="RN-195">picks from a list</Regra> you register, such as Department or Shift. An option that no longer applies is deactivated: people who already have it keep it.

The unit and sector that define what each person sees are still the **entities**. A "Unit" field is just data; it doesn't grant access to anything.

## What does each field option do?

- **Required in the profile:** people who haven't filled it in see a dialog when they sign in, including through corporate sign-in, and <Regra id="RN-197">can only use ITS after filling it in</Regra>. Administrators can leave it for later.
- **Searchable:** the user list search and the people picker also look in the field. Typing the employee ID finds the person.
- **Sensitive data:** the value <Regra id="RN-199">opens hidden, with the eye</Regra>, and never goes to exports, integrations or the people picker.
- **Only administrators change it once filled:** the person fills it in once, and after that only you change it. Useful for the employee ID, for example.
- **Goes to integrations:** the field shows in the Users module of the integration API. Sensitive data never goes out.
- **Unique value:** two people can't have the same value.

## Who fills them in?

Each person fills in <Regra id="RN-196">their own data</Regra> in **My account > My data**. You fill in and correct anyone's data in the user details, in the **Custom fields** section.

In the user list, people with an empty required field show the <Regra id="RN-198">Incomplete profile</Regra> badge, and the "Only incomplete profiles" filter shows how many there are. Each closed field also becomes a filter on the list.

## What about reports?

When someone submits an idea, ITS stores in it the values of the author's closed fields at that moment. In **Indicators**, the **Author field** filter and the "Ideas by Department" charts count each idea <Regra id="RN-201">by the value at the time of submission</Regra>: if the person changes department, their old ideas stay where they were. The queue export gets one column per closed field. Open fields don't go into reports.

## Can I change or delete a field?

You can rename a field and change its hint, limits and options at any time. Deactivating hides the field everywhere and keeps the values; reactivating brings it back. What you can't do is change the type or delete the field once someone has filled it in.

Drag the fields (or use the move up and down buttons) to change the order in which people see them.
