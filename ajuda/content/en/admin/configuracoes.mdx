# Settings and identity

Deadlines, modules, names and colors change from one organization to another without touching the code. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

## What goes in each Settings group?

- **Organization:** name, time zone, default language and Help address. The default language applies to the screens before signing in, to people you register and to emails sent to people without an account; each person changes their own in **My account**.
- **Ideas:** code prefix, required category, triage reply deadline (10 days, for example, calendar or business days) and other submission rules. Automatic assignment is under **Assignment**; see how the team uses both in [Queue and triage](/en/analista/triagem/).
- **Recognition (points and ranking)** and **Hall of fame:** turn the modules on and adjust the rules.
- **Idea wall:** comes turned off. <Regra id="RN-148">When it's on, Home becomes the wall</Regra>, and the improvement team publishes ideas on it. See [Publish and moderate the idea wall](/en/analista/mural/).
- **Store:** <Regra id="RN-159">only turns on with recognition on</Regra>. The <Regra id="RN-162">balance validity</Regra> is in months, and 0 means it never expires. See [Manage the store and bonuses](/en/admin/loja/).
- **Approval from other areas:** what happens <Regra id="RN-188">when someone doesn't approve</Regra> (return to the analyst, the factory setting, or block), the <Regra id="RN-185">confirmation code by email</Regra> (off by default), whether approvers with an email outside the company domains are accepted, and the default deadline. See [Request approval from another area](/en/analista/pedir-aprovacao/).
- **Steps and attachments:** how progress is calculated (by steps or by time) and the attachment types and size.
- **Public lookup and profile photo:** turn public lookup on and set the photo size.
- **Notifications and email:** how many days ahead deadline notifications go out, the daily summary time and the system address used in links.
- **Passwords and access:** password policy, attempts, lock, password link validity and session length, which is shorter for people who sign in with Microsoft or Google. Corporate sign-in itself is under [Corporate access](/en/admin/acesso-corporativo/).

## Which modules can I turn off?

Points, levels, achievements, ranking, likes, the Hall, the idea wall, the store and public lookup. The rest of ITS works without them, and nobody sees what's turned off.

## How do I give ITS my organization's look?

In **Visual identity**, choose the **Brand color** (ITS calculates the dark theme and, if needed, adjusts the tone so text stays readable), upload the **Organization logo**, which shows next to the ITS symbol, and choose the interface **Font**. The <Regra id="RN-176">sign-in screen</Regra> is there too, with its own layout, background (color, image or video) and texts. See [Customize the sign-in screen](/en/admin/tela-de-login/).

<Captura nome="identidade" alt="ITS visual identity screen, with the brand color picker and samples in the light and dark themes." />

With **Let each person choose the color and font of their own screen** turned on (it comes off), each person chooses in **My account** one of <Regra id="RN-145">8 colors checked for readability</Regra> and one of the 4 fonts. The logo, PDFs, emails and public screens keep the organization's identity.

## What if the Help is at another address?

By default, the Help lives at `/ajuda/`, on the same address as ITS. If it's hosted somewhere else, enter the path or an `https://` address in **Settings > Organization > Help address**.
