# Audit and privacy

ITS keeps what it needs to be transparent and protects what's personal.

## What goes into an idea's history?

Everything that happens to it: creation, status changes, steps, deadlines, assignments, internal comments and reopenings, always with who did it, when and why. <Regra id="RN-38">This history can't be erased</Regra>.

## What does the audit log record?

In **Audit**, you look up <Regra id="RN-39">sign-ins, failed attempts and changes to users, roles and settings</Regra>. That's where you answer "who changed this?". To find out whether the system is healthy, see [Reading observability](/en/admin/ler-a-observabilidade/).

## What about integrations and webhooks?

- From [integrations](/en/admin/integracoes/), the log gets the administrators' actions, such as creating, rotating a token and revoking, without the token value. <Regra id="RN-114">Reads made through the API aren't logged</Regra>.
- What an integration writes (submitting an idea, commenting, updating the reference, declaring a gain) <Regra id="RN-133">goes into the log</Regra>, with no person as author and without the comment text or the call body.
- From [webhooks](/en/admin/webhooks/), the log gets the switch, the destinations, the subscriptions and the secrets (only the server of the address, never the secret). <Regra id="RN-124">Each send itself is left out</Regra>.

## Why do some details show up hidden?

To protect against shared screens and people looking over your shoulder. The origin IP and the emails in each entry's details <Regra id="RN-135">open hidden</Regra>, and so do the settings fields that identify your organization: corporate sign-in, email and the webhook address. Click the **eye** to see them, and again to hide them.

While hidden, the value isn't even on the page. Once revealed, it hides again on its own when you change screens, when you save or after 60 seconds. It isn't a barrier against the administrator, and revealing isn't recorded in the audit log.

## How does ITS take care of personal data?

- **Disable and anonymize:** a disabled account keeps its history, and <Regra id="RN-40">anonymizing replaces the name and email with neutral values</Regra>, with no way back. See [Users, roles and scope](/en/admin/usuarios-e-perfis/).
- **Profile photo:** optional. ITS shrinks the image and deletes the metadata, such as the location. The <Regra id="RN-91">photo never shows in public lookup</Regra>, disappears from the Hall of fame and the ranking for people who hide their name, and is deleted on anonymization.
- **Name in the Hall and the ranking:** each person decides; see [Hall, points and privacy](/en/colaborador/hall-e-pontos/).
- **Public lookup:** never shows the author, comments, attachments, gains or personal data; see [Public lookup](/en/colaborador/consulta-publica/).

## Can anyone see another person's password?

Nobody can, not even the administrator. <Regra id="RN-42">Recovery always goes through a link that expires</Regra>, never through a password sent by email.
