# Store: trading points

When your organization <Regra id="RN-159">turns on the store</Regra>, **Store** shows up in the menu. There you trade points for the items and bonuses your organization offers.

## Does trading points lower my level?

No. They're <Regra id="RN-160">two different numbers</Regra>:

- **Points earned:** the ones you get from your ideas. They count for your level, achievements, the ranking and the Hall, and they never go down.
- **Balance:** what you spend in the store. <Regra id="RN-161">Every point you earn also goes into the balance</Regra>.

<Dica>
  When the store was turned on, your balance started with all the points you already had: that's the **Opening balance** line in your statement. A new ranking season doesn't reset the balance.
</Dica>

## Do points expire?

Only if your organization sets a <Regra id="RN-162">validity period</Regra>. In that case you get a notification 30 days ahead, saying how many points expire and when, and your oldest points are spent first. Points reserved in an order never expire.

## How do I order an item?

The **Catalog** tab has the <Regra id="RN-164">items available for your entity</Regra>. Click **Order** on the item. Each order is for one unit. The cost comes out of your balance and stays <Regra id="RN-165">reserved</Regra> until the store team decides.

## What happens after I order?

Follow it in the **My orders** tab. While the order is **Pending**, **Cancel order** gives the points back right away. If the team rejects or cancels it, <Regra id="RN-166">the points come back too</Regra>. Every decision about your order sends a notification to the bell, and <Regra id="RN-171">that notification always arrives</Regra>.

## What if it's a bonus?

A bonus is an item with a money value, like a voucher or a cash bonus. The value is for information only: once the order is approved, <Regra id="RN-167">HR pays it outside ITS</Regra> and marks it **Paid**. ITS doesn't pay anything or calculate tax; that's up to the organization.

## What shows up in my statement?

The **My statement** tab lists every credit and debit to your balance, newest first. <Regra id="RN-160">No line is ever deleted</Regra>: a correction always goes in as a new line. An **Adjustment** line is a credit or debit entered by the store team, always with the <Regra id="RN-168">reason</Regra>.

If an adjustment makes your balance negative, you <Regra id="RN-169">can't place new orders</Regra> until the balance covers the item. Orders you already placed go ahead, and you aren't charged anything.

## Who sees my balance?

Only <Regra id="RN-170">you and whoever manages the store</Regra>, and the same goes for your statement and your orders. The catalog shows how many times each item was ordered, never who ordered it.
