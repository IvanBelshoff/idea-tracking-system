# Hall, points and privacy

When an improvement works, ITS shows who made it happen, and you decide whether your name appears.

<Atencao>
  Your organization can turn off the Hall of fame, likes, points and the ranking. If something on this page doesn't show up for you, it's turned off.
</Atencao>

## How does an idea get into the Hall?

Someone with permission <Regra id="RN-72">picks the idea</Regra> and explains why it stands out. Only <Regra id="RN-73">implemented ideas</Regra> get in.

## Will my name appear?

Only if you want it to. When your idea is highlighted, you get a notification and <Regra id="RN-74">choose</Regra> between **Show my name** and **Keep me anonymous**. If you don't answer, you appear as "Anonymous collaborator".

To disappear from all boards and rankings, tick **Hide my name in boards and rankings** in **My account**: from then on you appear as <Regra id="RN-70">"Anonymous collaborator"</Regra>.

## What are likes for?

They show interest. Click **Like** on the card: <Regra id="RN-76">once per idea</Regra>, and never on your own idea. You can also leave praise, which everyone sees and which the team <Regra id="RN-77">can hide</Regra> if it doesn't belong.

## How do I earn points?

When recognition is turned on, you <Regra id="RN-66">earn points</Regra> when an idea of yours is submitted, approved, implemented and highlighted. Points lead to <Regra id="RN-67">levels</Regra> and <Regra id="RN-68">achievements</Regra>, and on an idea with co-authors <Regra id="RN-69">they're shared</Regra>. You can see all of this under **Recognition** in the menu.

Some organizations only show the ranking by entity, so it doesn't turn into a contest between coworkers.

If there's a cash prize or a gift, the organization arranges it <Regra id="RN-71">outside ITS</Regra>. If your organization turned on the store, see [Store: trading points](/en/colaborador/loja/).
