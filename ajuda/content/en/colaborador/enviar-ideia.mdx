# Submit an idea

Spotted something that could be better? <Regra id="RN-02">Any employee can submit</Regra> an idea, and it takes less than a minute.

## What do I need to fill in?

In the menu, choose **Submit idea**. Three fields are <Regra id="RN-03">required</Regra>:

- **Title:** one sentence that sums up the idea. "A label that does not peel off in the cold" says more than "Suggestion".
- **Current situation:** the problem (or the opportunity) and where it happens. You don't need a solution; a problem that's well explained already helps a lot.
- **Entity:** the site or department where this happens.

The **Category** (Safety, Quality, Cost…) may or may not be required; your organization decides. If there's an open campaign, it's highlighted on the form: you can <Regra id="RN-51">link the idea to the campaign</Regra>, but you don't have to. A proposed solution, expected gain and attachments are optional, but welcome. <Regra id="RN-136">A photo pasted into the text isn't kept</Regra>: send it as an attachment.

## Can I send a photo or a document?

Yes. Each idea takes up to 10 files: photos, videos or documents, in the types and size the administrator set. To check a file before you submit, click its name.

If a file doesn't upload, the idea goes through anyway: ITS shows the reason, and you can attach the file again on the idea page. Forgot a photo or sent the wrong one? While the idea is Submitted or In review, you can still <Regra id="RN-146">add and remove your files</Regra>. Once it's approved, the improvement team takes care of the files: if one is missing, ask for it in a comment.

## What if someone already had the same idea?

As you type, ITS <Regra id="RN-57">shows open ideas similar</Regra> to yours. If one of them asks for the same thing, click **Support this idea**: you become a co-author, and the team doesn't have to review the same request twice. If none of them matches, submit yours. See [Support an idea](/en/colaborador/apoiar/).

## What happens after I submit?

- The idea gets a number, like `ITS-2026-0043`, and starts as **Submitted**.
- Right away, you see the **Expected reply**: <Regra id="RN-54">the date by which the team should answer</Regra> for the first time.
- When an analyst takes on the review, you're notified. The rest is in [Follow your idea](/en/colaborador/acompanhar/).

<Dica>
  Would you rather have someone type it for you? An analyst can register the idea on your behalf, and it's still yours: ITS <Regra id="RN-04">records you as the author</Regra>, along with who registered it.
</Dica>
