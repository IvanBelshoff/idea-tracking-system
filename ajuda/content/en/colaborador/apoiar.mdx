# Support an idea

Has someone already asked for the same improvement? Support the existing idea instead of submitting another one just like it: the team doesn't review the same request twice, and you follow everything.

## Where is the button?

Most often, right when you submit: as you type, ITS <Regra id="RN-57">shows open ideas similar</Regra> to yours, each with **Support this idea**. Try it in the [Submit an idea](/en/colaborador/enviar-ideia/) simulator.

## What changes when I support an idea?

You become a <Regra id="RN-86">co-author</Regra>: the idea goes into **My ideas**, and you <Regra id="RN-87">get notified</Regra> of every change. Whoever submitted it first is still the author, and the team sees how many people asked for the same thing, which helps it decide what to do first.

You can only support an idea while it's open (Submitted, In review or In progress). Once it's finished, new co-authors can only be added by the team.

<Dica>
  Have a detail the original idea leaves out? When you support it, write it in **Anything to add**. Only the team reviewing the idea sees that text.
</Dica>
