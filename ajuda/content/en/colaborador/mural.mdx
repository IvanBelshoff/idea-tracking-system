# Idea wall

When your organization <Regra id="RN-148">turns on the idea wall</Regra>, **Home** shows the improvements happening across the company, with your shortcuts alongside under **My ideas and tasks**.

## Which ideas show up there?

Only the ones the <Regra id="RN-149">improvement team published</Regra>, and only after they're approved (In progress or Implemented). Before that, an idea may still end up not approved. Your idea doesn't go to the wall on its own.

Everyone who signs in to ITS sees the wall, from any site or department. That's why the card shows only a little: title, status, category, entity and reactions. The idea's <Regra id="RN-150">description, attachments, gain and comments</Regra> stay off the wall.

## How do I react?

React with one click. You get <Regra id="RN-152">one reaction per idea</Regra>, and you can change or remove it later.

<Dica>
  When you react, your name appears in the list of who reacted. Reacting gives no points and doesn't change anything in the idea's review.
</Dica>

You can't react to an idea you're the author or a co-author of. That way, the list of who reacted never gives away an author who chose to stay anonymous.

## How do I comment?

Open the idea on the wall to see the **Conversation**. It's <Regra id="RN-153">separate from the idea's comments</Regra>: what you write in one place doesn't show up in the other. Your comment is published right away, with your name and photo.

## What if a comment goes too far?

If it's offensive, contains personal or confidential data, or is off topic, click **Report** and choose the reason. The improvement team reviews it, and <Regra id="RN-154">the writer never learns who reported it</Regra>.

## How do I share?

Under **Share**, use **Copy link** (the link only opens for people who sign in to ITS) or **Send to a colleague**, who gets the notification in the bell. <Regra id="RN-155">Nothing leaves the organization</Regra>: there are no social media buttons and no public link.

## Does my name appear?

Only if you want it to. When an idea of yours (or one you co-author) is published, you get a notification, and the **Idea wall** block on the idea page asks whether you want to show your name. Until you choose **Show my name**, you appear as "Author of the idea" (or "Co-author of the idea"), <Regra id="RN-151">without a photo</Regra>, even in your comments. The improvement team still knows who you are. You can change your mind at any time.

<Dica>
  Did you tick **Hide my name in boards and rankings** in My account? Then your name doesn't appear as the author on the wall, even if you accept. On other ideas, your reactions and comments still show your name, because those actions are your own.
</Dica>

## What notifications does the wall send?

The wall has <Regra id="RN-156">its own notifications</Regra>, like the daily summary for your idea ("5 reactions and 2 comments today") and replies to your comment. You can turn all of them off in **My account > Notifications**, in the **Idea wall** group.
