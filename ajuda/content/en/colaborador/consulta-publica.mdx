# Public lookup

Want to show your idea's progress to someone who doesn't use ITS? Turn on public lookup: anyone with the link can follow the idea without signing in.

<Atencao>
  Public lookup is off by default. If the idea details say "Public lookup is turned off by the administration", your organization <Regra id="RN-33">hasn't turned the feature on</Regra>.
</Atencao>

## How do I turn it on?

1. Open the idea and tick **Allow public lookup for this idea**. <Regra id="RN-37">It's your call</Regra>, idea by idea.
2. Send the **Tracking link** to anyone you want.

The code in the link is long and random: <Regra id="RN-34">nobody can guess</Regra> another idea's code, and anyone who keeps trying codes runs into an <Regra id="RN-36">attempt limit</Regra>.

## What does the person see?

<Regra id="RN-35">Only the title, category, status, steps</Regra> (name, due date and completion) and the deadline. They never see who submitted or supports the idea, the comments, the attachments, the gains or any personal data.

<Dica>
  Changed your mind? Untick the option and the link stops working.
</Dica>
