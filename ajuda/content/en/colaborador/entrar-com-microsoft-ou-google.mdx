# Sign in with Microsoft or Google

If your organization turned on corporate access, the sign-in screen has a **Sign in with Microsoft** or **Sign in with Google** button. You use your company email account and don't need another password.

## How do I sign in?

Click the button and sign in with your company account, the same way you do in other systems. If it asks you to confirm on your phone, it's the same confirmation as always. Then ITS opens, and if you were on your way to a screen, it takes you back there.

The first time, ITS <Regra id="RN-96">recognizes you by your email</Regra>: if your email is already registered, you sign in with your usual roles and ideas. If it isn't, an account is created on the spot only if your organization uses <Regra id="RN-98">automatic creation</Regra>, with the role and entity the administrator set.

## Something went wrong. What now?

If you see "This account's email is not from one of the organization's domains" or "This account does not belong to the organization", you signed in with another account, maybe your personal one: try again with your company account. If you see "Your email is not registered in ITS yet", ask the administrator to register your email. For any other message, do what it says; if the error continues, let the administrator know.

## What about the password?

It depends on the organization. In some, the password still works alongside the button; in others, only the company account can sign in, and then <Regra id="RN-97">there's no password</Regra> to create or forget. Consultants and contractors, who have an email from outside the company, always sign in with a password.
