# Results and standardization

Once the idea is implemented, what's left is showing what changed and making sure the improvement sticks. Everything here is optional, but it's what proves that a good idea delivered results.

## How do I show what changed?

Record the **Indicator**, the unit, the **Baseline** (the value before), the **Target** and the **Achieved** value. With a target, ITS <Regra id="RN-61">shows how much of it was reached</Regra>. For example: "Tool changeover time, in minutes: baseline 40, target 15, achieved 12".

## What about the financial gain?

The gain is <Regra id="RN-62">estimated during triage and realized at completion</Regra>, with its periodicity (one-off, monthly or yearly) and the implementation cost.

The realized gain stays **Declared** until someone with permission to validate, such as the finance team, confirms it. Then it becomes **Validated**, and <Regra id="RN-63">only validated gains count in the official indicators and in the Hall of fame</Regra>.

## Where do the lesson learned and replication come in?

When you implement, record the standard adopted and whether the solution is **Replicable**. If it is, you replicate the idea in another entity, and ITS <Regra id="RN-64">creates a new idea linked to the original</Regra>.

## Did the improvement hold?

Your organization can require a <Regra id="RN-65">sustainment review</Regra>, for example 90 days later: the owner gets a reminder and confirms whether the gain still holds.

## How do I discuss the review without the author seeing it?

Use <Regra id="RN-28">internal comments</Regra>. And everything that changes on the idea stays in its timeline, with who did it, when and why.
