# Publish and moderate the idea wall

The idea wall shows the whole organization the improvements that are happening. The improvement team decides what goes on it: no idea goes to the wall on its own.

## How is the wall turned on?

The wall comes **off**, and whoever administers ITS turns it on under **Settings > Idea wall**. When it's on, everyone's Home becomes the wall. When it's off, everything leaves the screen and <Regra id="RN-148">is kept for when it's turned back on</Regra>.

## How do I publish an idea?

You need the **Publish on the idea wall and moderate the conversation** permission, which comes with the Analyst and Administrator roles and applies to the entities in your scope. On the idea page, the **Idea wall** block shows whether the idea is on the wall. To publish it, use **Publish on the wall**. You can only publish an idea that is <Regra id="RN-149">In progress or Implemented</Regra>.

## What shows up on the wall?

The card carries <Regra id="RN-150">only the title, status, category and entity</Regra>. The author and co-authors get a notification and <Regra id="RN-151">stay anonymous until they agree</Regra> to show their name. The team always sees who they are, with the name marked "visible only to the team".

<Dica>
  Whoever publishes takes care of confidentiality. ITS doesn't mark any category or entity as confidential: if the idea shouldn't be seen by the whole organization, don't publish it. The confirmation reminds you of this before you publish.
</Dica>

## What about removing an idea?

**Remove from the wall** asks for a reason. Reactions, the conversation and consents are kept, and they come back if the idea is published again. Interrupting or archiving the idea removes it automatically; reopening doesn't publish it again. Publishing and removing are recorded in the idea history and in the audit log.

## How do I moderate the conversation?

In the conversation of an idea on the wall, each comment shows <Regra id="RN-153">**Hide** and **Restore**</Regra> and, if it was edited, **Previous versions**. Hiding asks for a reason: the writer is notified and sees the reason, and everyone else reads "Comment hidden by the team".

## Where are the reports?

In the **Idea wall reports** menu, in the improvement team group. It gathers the reported comments of the entities you moderate, with the reasons and details of each report, but <Regra id="RN-154">without saying who reported</Regra>. For each comment:

- **Hide** hides the comment and resolves its reports.
- **Dismiss** keeps the comment visible and takes its reports out of the queue.

Hiding, restoring and dismissing are recorded in the audit log, without the comment text. When new reports come in, you get <Regra id="RN-156">one notification per day that groups them</Regra>.

## What about integrations?

Systems integrated with ITS can be notified when an idea goes on or comes off the wall (see [Webhooks: setting up](/en/admin/webhooks/)), and the read API shows whether the idea is on the wall. Reactions, comments and reports <Regra id="RN-157">never leave through the integration</Regra>.

## What about accessibility?

The wall loads in pages and <Regra id="RN-158">works entirely with the keyboard</Regra>, in both themes. Each reaction is a button with a name and a state, and screen readers announce the count.
