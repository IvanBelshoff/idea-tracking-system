# Approve and run steps

Once approved, the idea becomes work, done in steps: one at a time, each with an owner and a date.

## What do I do when I approve?

You <Regra id="RN-06">set up the first step and choose its owner</Regra>. Steps can be free or follow the **PDCA** model (Plan, Do, Check, Act). The phase is only a label, and <Regra id="RN-60">the step rules are the same</Regra>.

<Dica>
  An idea that is still Submitted can also be approved directly. ITS records both moves in the timeline: taking the review and approving.
</Dica>

## How do steps work?

Each step has a <Regra id="RN-15">name, description, owner, start, due date and attachments</Regra>, and the <Regra id="RN-16">due date can't be before the start</Regra>. The <Regra id="RN-12">next step can only be created once the previous one is completed</Regra>. A completed step <Regra id="RN-13">can't be edited or deleted</Regra>; only the last one, while still open, can change.

The idea's deadline is the due date of its last step: <Regra id="RN-17">a new step extends the deadline</Regra>, and the change stays in the history. <Regra id="RN-19">Progress is the share of completed steps</Regra> or, if your organization prefers, the elapsed time.

## When is an idea late?

The idea becomes **Late** when its open step passes the due date; <Regra id="RN-18">on the due date itself, it's still on time</Regra>. The owner and the analyst <Regra id="RN-29">are notified a few days before</Regra> and on the due date.

## How do I close the idea?

When you complete the last step, ITS asks whether you want to <Regra id="RN-20">create another step or mark the idea as implemented</Regra>. To mark it **Implemented**, <Regra id="RN-07">every step must be completed, and you describe the result achieved</Regra>. Measurement, gains and lessons learned are in [Results and standardization](/en/analista/resultado/).

If the work has to stop halfway, use **Interrupt**: the idea becomes Not approved, also with a justification.

<Atencao>
  Attachments follow the types and maximum size set by the administrator, and <Regra id="RN-21">can only be removed while the step is open</Regra>. <Regra id="RN-147">Attached photos and videos open right on the page</Regra>; other files are downloaded.
</Atencao>
