# Queue and triage

Triage is the first reply the author gets. ITS helps you make sure nobody goes without an answer, even when the queue is full.

## Where are the ideas that came in?

The **Idea queue** has the ideas from the entities you follow, each with its status, how long it has been waiting and its **Expected reply** date.

<Captura nome="fila" alt="ITS idea queue, with status, entity and category filters and the list of ideas with their expected reply dates." />

## How long do I have to reply?

Your organization sets the <Regra id="RN-54">deadline for the first reply</Regra>, for example 10 days, counted as calendar days or business days only. The author sees the expected date from the moment they submit.

If the idea is still **Submitted** after the expected date, it gets the **Reply overdue** flag, and the analyst and the manager are notified. Lateness starts the day after the expected date and goes away when someone takes the review.

## Who reviews each idea?

The review can go to someone <Regra id="RN-55">automatically or by hand</Regra>. Automatic assignment follows entity and category rules, such as "Safety at Site A goes to Ana", which are set in **Administration > Assignment**.

## What comes first?

During triage, rate **Impact** and **Effort** from 1 to 5. <Regra id="RN-56">Priority is impact divided by effort</Regra>, and the queue can be sorted by it. It's only there to help: you make the decision.

## What do I decide in triage?

- **Take the review:** the idea moves to In review, and the author is notified.
- **Do not approve:** <Regra id="RN-05">always with a justification</Regra>, which goes to the author. Write it the way you'd like to read it: the reason and, if there is one, what could change.

To approve, see [Approve and run steps](/en/analista/aprovar-e-etapas/).

## Can I handle several ideas at once?

Yes. <Regra id="RN-59">Select several in the queue</Regra> to assign them, change their category or reject them all with the same justification. You can also save the filters you use often.
