# Welcome to the ITS Help

ITS is where improvement ideas stop being hallway talk and become real work. This Help shows how each part works and lets you practice before you use it day to day.

## ITS in one sentence

Anyone submits an idea, the improvement team reviews it and carries it out in steps, the person who submitted it follows every step, and the best ideas are featured in the Hall of fame.

All of this exists because nobody likes sending a suggestion and never hearing about it again. In ITS, an idea always has a status that the person who submitted it can see, a deadline and a next step.

## How this Help works

Each page answers, in short texts, a question from someone using the system. Along the way you'll find:

- **"Practice" boxes.** They're simulators with made-up data. Click as much as you like: nothing that happens there reaches the real ITS.
- **Linked rules.** Every ITS behavior comes from a numbered business rule. In the text, the rule shows up as a linked word or phrase, as in "when an idea isn't approved, <Regra id="RN-05">the justification goes to the person who submitted it</Regra>". The link takes you to the rule, for when you want to understand why.

## Names and colors

Each organization sets up ITS its own way: the color, the logo, the font and some names may be different in the system you use. It works the same way, and this Help uses the default names.
