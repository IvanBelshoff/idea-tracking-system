# Highlight ideas in the Hall

The Hall of fame recognizes the best ideas. Whoever has curation permission chooses what goes in, and ITS handles the rules: what can be highlighted, who appears by name and until when.

## Who can highlight ideas?

Highlighting in the **entity** Hall and in the **organization** Hall are <Regra id="RN-79">different permissions</Regra>. By default, managers highlight in their entity, and organization highlights stay with the administrator, who can give that permission to other roles.

## Which ideas qualify?

Only **Implemented** ideas <Regra id="RN-73">can be highlighted</Regra>. In progress ideas only if your organization allows it. Not approved ideas, never.

## How do I highlight an idea?

Click **Highlight an idea** in the Hall, or **Highlight in the Hall** in the idea details. In **Why it stands out**, write the justification: <Regra id="RN-72">it appears on the card</Regra>.

In **Start** and **End**, choose the highlight period. When it ends, <Regra id="RN-78">the idea moves to that year's Hall of fame</Regra>. To take it down earlier, use **Revoke highlight** and give the reason.

## Who appears by name?

When you highlight an idea, <Regra id="RN-80">the author and co-authors are notified</Regra> and choose whether their names appear. <Regra id="RN-74">If they don't answer, they appear as "Anonymous collaborator"</Regra>. Anyone who ticked **Hide my name in boards and rankings** in **My account** also appears that way.

## Does ITS suggest ideas for the Hall?

Yes, if your organization sets criteria, such as validated gain above an amount or a number of likes. Ideas that meet the criteria show up in **Suggestions for the Hall**, and the curators decide. <Regra id="RN-72">They only go in on their own</Regra> if the administrator turns that option on.

## Can I hide a piece of praise?

Praise in the Hall is public, but <Regra id="RN-77">whoever moderates can hide a piece of praise</Regra>, giving the reason. By default, the moderator is the analyst.
