# Approve an idea

Your organization's improvement team wants your approval before moving on with an idea that affects your area. Answering takes a few minutes, and you don't need an ITS account.

## I got an email with a link. What now?

Open the link. The page <Regra id="RN-184">opens without signing in</Regra> and shows only what the team chose for you: the idea's code and title, the summary, the area you represent, who asked, the deadline and, sometimes, the estimated gain and a few attachments.

Choose **Approve** or **Do not approve**, confirm your name and send. If you don't approve, write the reason: it helps the team adjust the idea.

<Dica>
  The link works **once**, only for you, until the deadline. Don't forward the email: whoever opens the link answers in your name.
</Dica>

## The page asked for a code. What is it?

Some organizations ask for an extra confirmation. Click **Email me a code**: a <Regra id="RN-185">6-digit code</Regra> arrives at the same email and is valid for 10 minutes. Got it wrong five times, or did the code expire? Ask for another one.

## I have an ITS account. Where do I answer?

You don't get a link: the request arrives in the bell, and you answer in <Regra id="RN-186">**Pending approvals**</Regra>, in the menu, with the same summary. The **Open the idea** button only appears if you could already see the idea before.

## What happens after I answer?

The page shows **Answer recorded**, and the link stops working. Your answer doesn't change the idea on its own: the improvement team decides the next step. If you don't answer, you get <Regra id="RN-189">a reminder</Regra> 3 days before the deadline and another on the day.

Does the link say it's no longer valid? The deadline passed, the team cancelled the request or it was already closed. If you still want to answer, talk to the improvement team.

## I answered by email or in a meeting. Does it count?

Yes. The team can <Regra id="RN-187">record your decision</Regra> with evidence, such as the email or the minutes.
