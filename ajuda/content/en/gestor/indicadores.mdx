# Indicators and reports

The **Indicators** dashboard shows whether the idea program is moving: how many ideas come in, how long the team takes to reply and to deliver, who takes part and what results the improvements bring.

<Captura nome="indicadores" alt="ITS indicators dashboard, with period, entity and category filters, counts by status and process indicators." />

## How do I filter the numbers?

Choose the period (**From** and **To**), the **Entity** and the **Category**, and <Regra id="RN-44">everything on the dashboard follows</Regra>. You <Regra id="RN-49">only see numbers for the entities in your scope</Regra>, so two managers from different areas see different dashboards.

If your organization has closed profile fields, such as the department, the **Author field** filter and the "Ideas by…" charts count each idea <Regra id="RN-201">by the value at the time of submission</Regra>: if the person moves to another department later, their earlier ideas stay where they were.

## What does each block show?

- **Ideas by status:** <Regra id="RN-44">how many ideas came in and how many are in each status</Regra>.
- **Process indicators:** the <Regra id="RN-45">average triage time</Regra> (from submission to decision) and the average execution time (from approval to implementation), the approval rate, the share of late ideas and **Ideas per author**, the average among the people who submitted.
- **Engagement:** <Regra id="RN-81">participation</Regra> (how many active people submitted at least one idea), **Ideas per active collaborator**, which also counts those who submitted none, the implementation rate and the month-by-month trend.
- **Financial result:** the <Regra id="RN-46">accumulated gain by category and entity</Regra> and the <Regra id="RN-82">return</Regra>, which is the validated gain divided by the implementation cost. Only **validated** gains count here; see [Validate gains](/en/gestor/ganhos/).

<Dica>
  Prefer to see the numbers, or use a screen reader? The **Monthly trend** chart has **View as table**.
</Dica>

## How do I get a report?

For one idea, open its details and click **Download PDF**: <Regra id="RN-47">the report has the idea's data, steps and status</Regra>. **PDF with history** also includes the timeline.

For a list, use **Export CSV** in the **Idea queue**. The file has <Regra id="RN-48">exactly what the filters show</Regra>, ready to open in a spreadsheet.
