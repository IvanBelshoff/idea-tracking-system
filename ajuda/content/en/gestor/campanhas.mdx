# Campaigns

A campaign is a challenge with a deadline, such as "ideas to cut delivery time by March". It gives the program a focus for a period.

## How do I create a campaign?

In **Campaigns**, click **New campaign** and fill in the <Regra id="RN-50">name, objective, period, target entities and categories, and goal</Regra>. The goal can be a number of ideas or a result, such as "cut delivery time by 15%". If there's a reward, enter it too: it's informational only, and ITS pays nothing.

The campaign starts as a draft. When it's ready, click **Activate**, and it shows up on the home page and in the submission form.

Creating campaigns requires permission. By default, managers and administrators have it.

## Do people have to pick a campaign when they submit an idea?

No. While a campaign is active, whoever submits can <Regra id="RN-51">link the idea to the campaign</Regra>, but submitting without a campaign is always possible: the idea follows the continuous flow.

## How do I follow the campaign?

Each campaign has a dashboard with the ideas received, approved and implemented, the participants and <Regra id="RN-52">the accumulated gain compared with the goal</Regra>.

## What happens when I close it?

**Close campaign** saves the summary and <Regra id="RN-53">suggests ideas for the Hall of fame</Regra>. The curators still decide what goes in; see [Highlight ideas in the Hall](/en/gestor/hall/).
