# Validate gains

When an idea is implemented, the team reports how much it saved or earned. Before that amount counts officially, someone who knows the numbers, such as the finance team, checks and confirms it.

## What's the difference between declared and validated?

The **Declared** gain is what the team reported at completion, <Regra id="RN-62">with periodicity and implementation cost</Regra>. It becomes **Validated** when someone with permission to validate confirms the amount. <Regra id="RN-63">Only validated gains count in the official indicators</Regra> and show up in the Hall of fame, with a badge.

## Where do I validate?

**Gains to validate** lists the ideas in your scope with a declared gain waiting for confirmation. Open the idea, check the calculation and click **Validate gain**. If the amount is wrong, adjust it before validating.

<Atencao>
  If the validated amount changes later, the gain goes back to "declared" until someone validates it again.
</Atencao>

## Who validates?

ITS comes with the **Results validator** role, which has permission to validate and to see the indicators. It doesn't triage or run ideas: it only confirms numbers. The full matrix is in [Roles and permissions](/en/referencia/perfis-e-permissoes/).
