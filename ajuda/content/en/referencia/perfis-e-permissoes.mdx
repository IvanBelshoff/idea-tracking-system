# Roles and permissions

A permission allows an action in ITS, such as submitting ideas, triaging ideas, validating gains or seeing system health. A role is a set of permissions, and each person is given a role.

Every role applies in a scope: an entity (a part of the organization's structure, such as a site or a department), either that entity alone or its sub-entities too, which sit below it. That's why someone can be an employee in one department and an analyst at another site. The person who submits an idea always sees it, whatever their role. See [Users, roles and scope](/en/admin/usuarios-e-perfis/).

ITS comes with five factory roles: **Employee**, **Analyst**, **Manager**, **Results validator** and **Administrator**. The table shows what each one can do on a fresh installation. The administrator can edit these roles and create others, so they may be different in your organization.
