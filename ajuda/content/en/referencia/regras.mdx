# Business rules

Every ITS behavior comes from a numbered business rule, such as RN-05. They're all here, in plain language, each with a link to the page where you see it at work.

In the Help text, a rule shows up as a linked word or phrase, as in "when an idea isn't approved, <Regra id="RN-05">the justification goes to the person who submitted it</Regra>". The link brings you to the rule, on this page.
