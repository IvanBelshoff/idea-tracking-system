# Corporate sign-in

This page is for whoever installs and maintains ITS and looks after your organization's Microsoft or Google. Whoever administers ITS uses the [Corporate access](/en/admin/acesso-corporativo/) screen; this page covers what must exist **before** it.

ITS uses **OpenID Connect**, server to server. With the feature off (the default), ITS doesn't talk to anything outside. When it's on, the API only talks to the [provider](/en/referencia/glossario/#provedor) when someone signs in: to discover the addresses, exchange the code and read the public keys.

<Dica>
  To send email through Microsoft 365, use **a different application**, only for sending and restricted to one mailbox. See [Sending email through Microsoft](/en/ti/envio-pela-microsoft/).
</Dica>

## What do I need to prepare first?

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **System address** on `https://` (in **Settings > Notifications and email**) | Microsoft and Google only accept `http` on `localhost`. The [return address](/en/referencia/glossario/#endereco-de-retorno) is built from it |
| **`APP_SECRET_KEY`** in `.env` | The client secret is stored encrypted with it. If the key changes, register the secret again |
| **Outbound network access** from the API server | To `login.microsoftonline.com` (Microsoft), `accounts.google.com` and `www.googleapis.com` (Google), on port 443 |
| **Organization domains** registered | Corporate sign-in only works for emails from those domains |

### What is the return address?

It's the system address plus the provider's path:

- Microsoft: `https://ideas.yourcompany.com/api/auth/sso/microsoft/callback`
- Google: `https://ideas.yourcompany.com/api/auth/sso/google/callback`

The **Corporate access** screen shows the exact address, ready to copy. The address registered with the provider must be **identical** to it.

### What if the server goes out through a proxy?

If the server only reaches the internet through a proxy, set this in `.env`:

```bash
NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1
HTTPS_PROXY=http://proxy.yourcompany.local:3128
NO_PROXY=localhost,127.0.0.1,db,api
```

Then bring the services up again (`docker compose ... up -d`). Without a proxy, set nothing.

## How do I register ITS with Microsoft (Entra ID)?

<Regra id="RN-96">ITS works with a single tenant</Regra>, your organization's.

1. In the **Microsoft Entra admin center**, open **Applications > App registrations > New registration**.
2. Name: `ITS`. Under **Supported account types**, choose **Accounts in this organizational directory only (single tenant)**.
3. Under **Redirect URI**, choose the **Web** platform and enter the Microsoft return address.
4. Under **Overview**, copy the **Application (client) ID** and the **Directory (tenant) ID**. On the ITS screen, they go in **Client (application) ID** and **Tenant (directory) ID**.
5. Under **Certificates & secrets > New client secret**, create a secret and copy the **Value** (not the secret ID). It's shown **only once**.
6. Under **Token configuration > Add optional claim**, choose the **ID** token type and select `email`. That way the token carries the email.
7. To limit **who** can sign in: under **Enterprise applications > ITS > Properties**, turn on **Assignment required** and, under **Users and groups**, add the authorized group.

The default permissions (`openid`, `profile`, `email`) are enough; ITS reads nothing beyond the sign-in.

If the ITS administrator turns on **Offer the profile photo**, add the delegated permission **Microsoft Graph > User.Read** to the application (under *API permissions*). Without it, sign-in keeps working, but the photo doesn't come. ITS fetches the photo from the server, only at `graph.microsoft.com`, and <Regra id="RN-144">doesn't keep the access token</Regra>.

## How do I register ITS with Google?

1. In the **Google Cloud Console**, choose (or create) a project and open **APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen**.
2. Under **User type**, choose **Internal**, which restricts access to your Google Workspace, and fill in the app name and the support email.
3. Under **Credentials > Create credentials > OAuth client ID**, choose the **Web application** type.
4. Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, enter the Google return address.
5. Copy the **Client ID** and the **Client secret**.
6. To limit **who** can sign in, control access to the app by **organizational unit** in the **Google Workspace Admin Console**.

ITS only accepts Workspace accounts from the **registered domains**. A personal account (gmail.com) can't sign in.

## What do I do when the secret expires?

The [client secret](/en/referencia/glossario/) **expires**. On Microsoft, it lasts 24 months at most. Note the date and enter it in **Secret expiry date**, on the **Corporate access** screen, so ITS health warns you before it expires.

When the secret expires, corporate sign-in stops working. Create a new secret at the provider and register it in **Corporate access**. Meanwhile, people with [emergency access](/en/referencia/glossario/#acesso-de-emergencia) (the administrators) keep signing in with a password. Replacing the secret turns the provider off: after saving the new one, run the test and click **Turn on**, and corporate sign-in is back to normal.

## What do I do when sign-in fails?

The reason appears on the sign-in screen and is recorded in **Audit** as `Corporate account sign-in refused`.

| Message or symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| "The provider did not finish the sign-in" | Is the registered return address **identical** to the one on the screen? Has the secret expired? See `logs api` |
| "We could not reach the provider" | Outbound network access to the provider (firewall and proxy). Use **Test address** on the screen |
| "This account does not belong to the organization" | Is the account from another [tenant](/en/referencia/glossario/#tenant)? Is the tenant ID on the screen the one for the right directory? |
| "This account's email is not from one of the organization's domains" | Is the domain on the list? On Google, is the account a Workspace account and not a personal one? |
| "Your email is not registered in ITS yet" | Register the person under **Users** or turn on automatic creation |
| Everyone refused after changing `APP_SECRET_KEY` | ITS can no longer read the registered secret: register it again |
| Nobody can sign in in **SSO only** mode | An administrator signs in with a password, changes the mode to **Password only** and fixes the problem |

<Atencao>
  If the provider goes down, ITS **can't** warn about it on its own. Administrators keep signing in with a password. To follow the installation's health, see [Operation and alerts](/en/ti/operacao-e-alertas/).
</Atencao>
